DISCRETE STATE SPACE 12 States · 3 Actions Traffic × Logins × Behavior
01

Discrete State Space (12 States)

Network traffic is encoded as $\mathbf{s} = \langle \text{Traffic Intensity}, \text{Failed Logins}, \text{Connection Behavior} \rangle$. Spans benign states (0, 4, 8), suspicious probes (1, 2, 5, 6), and severe exploits (3, 7, 9, 10, 11).

02

Action Space & Trade-offs

MONITOR (0): +5 on benign; -12 on severe attack.
INVESTIGATE (1): +8 on suspicious probe; -2 on benign (triage overhead).
BLOCK (2): +10 on severe attack; -8 on benign (false alarm business disruption).

03

Three Core RL Approaches

Q-Learning: Off-policy Bellman optimality target $\max_{a'} Q(s', a')$.
SARSA: On-policy actual next action target $Q(s', a')$.
Model-Based RL: Learns empirical $\hat{P}(s'|s,a)$ and $\hat{R}$ with Value Iteration planning.

04

Rigorous Evaluation Protocol

Evaluation runs on separate seeded episodes with zero learning: Q-tables, transition counts, and value estimates are frozen to guarantee unbiased measurement.

EDUCATIONAL SYLLABUS & VIVA GUIDE

Reinforcement Learning Fundamentals in Cyber Defense

THEORETICAL FOUNDATION
01

Markov Decision Process (MDP)

An MDP is represented as the tuple $(S, A, P, R, \gamma)$: $S$: Discrete states (12 feature combinations).
$A$: Actions (Monitor, Investigate, Block).
$P(s'|s,a)$: Transition probability function.
$R(s,a,s')$: Scalar feedback reward.
$\gamma$: Discount factor ($0 \le \gamma < 1$).

02

Q-Learning (Off-Policy TD)

Updates state-action values using the Bellman Optimality target: $$Q(s, a) \leftarrow Q(s, a) + \alpha \left[ r + \gamma \max_{a'} Q(s', a') - Q(s, a) \right]$$ Why Off-Policy? The target utilizes the maximum future Q-value over all actions, independent of the action chosen by the behavior policy.

03

SARSA (On-Policy TD)

Updates state-action values using the action actually selected by the current policy: $$Q(s, a) \leftarrow Q(s, a) + \alpha \left[ r + \gamma Q(s', a') - Q(s, a) \right]$$ Why On-Policy? SARSA estimates the return of the policy currently being executed, taking the risk of exploration into account.

04

Model-Based RL & Planning

Learns an empirical model $\hat{P}(s'|s,a) = \frac{C(s,a,s')}{N(s,a)}$ and performs Value Iteration planning: $$V(s) \leftarrow \max_{a} \sum_{s'} \hat{P}(s'|s,a) \left[ \hat{R} + \gamma V(s') \right]$$ Enables policy derivation with far fewer real environment interactions.
