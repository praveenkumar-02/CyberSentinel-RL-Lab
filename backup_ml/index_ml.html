SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

How the Detection System Works

AI PIPELINE
01

Data Ingestion & Sanitization

Network telemetry rows are ingested from CSV streams. Column aliasing maps varied schemas into standard flow metrics: duration, protocol, packets, bytes, and failed_logins.

02

Leakage-Free Preprocessing

A 25% stratified test split isolates unseen records prior to model fitting. A ColumnTransformer applies median imputation and StandardScaler to numeric attributes, with OneHotEncoder expanding categorical protocols.

03

Supervised Classification

Decision Tree: White-box tree splitting on Gini impurity to expose clear signature thresholds.
Random Forest: Ensemble of 100 decorrelated trees with bootstrap aggregation and balanced class weighting.

04

Unsupervised Anomaly Detection

Isolation Forest: Recursively partitions feature space. Anomalous flows require significantly fewer random splits to isolate. Outlier predictions (-1) map to suspicious flags without needing prior training labels.

Prototype & Educational Disclaimer

CyberSentinel AI is designed for cyber defense education, algorithm benchmarking, and threat visualization. Synthetic traffic demonstrations reproduce known volumetric and brute-force signatures but do not guarantee protection against real-world zero-day exploits. In operational environments, intrusion detection systems (IDS) require deep packet inspection, TLS fingerprinting, and expert SOC review.
